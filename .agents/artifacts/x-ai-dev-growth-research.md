# Building an X audience of AI developers

Date accessed: 2026-10-06. Research only. No posts, replies, likes, or DMs were sent. No site code was changed.

Audience: people who build coding agents, harnesses, evals, and tools. Publishing assumption: 1–2 technical blogs a week, plus X posts that can stand without a blog.

## Week shape

Publish the blog. Post one X version that states the result in the first lines and links it. Add an independent post only for a new artifact or a specific contribution to a conversation already open: a PR, a prompt or trace, a measurement, or a quote that adds an operating rule. Reply as yourself. Leave empty days empty.

A fixed 3–5 X posts a week is not supported. A keyword search for weekly posting advice since 2026-01-01 (`min_faves:10`) returned a coaching email playbook and a YouTube ROI post, both outside this audience. No AI-dev post retrieved here set that quota. There is no universal optimum in this evidence.

Volume still moves. Hamel Husain sliced one FAQ into several posts in a release. Simon Willison posted several notes in four days because the notes existed. Paul Iusztin's filtered sample is four substantial posts across about eleven weeks.

## For You code, and only that

README of [xai-org/x-algorithm](https://github.com/xai-org/x-algorithm), read 2026-10-06. It describes For You. Weights scale predicted probabilities, including follow, reply, repost, dwell, and negative actions. The README warns that a weight ratio is easy to misread as "one report cancels N likes." Weights were not copied.

- `AgeFilter` drops posts older than 48 hours before scoring. Each post's For You window is short. The filter does not choose a weekly count.
- Author diversity multiplies later posts from the same author, inside one ranking, by a decaying factor down to a floor. Several posts in the same 48 hours compete. The factor is not stated.
- `OONRetweetReplyFilter` removes reposts and replies from accounts the viewer does not follow. The out-of-network discount also covers replies and reposts from accounts they do follow. Quote-posts in this sample carried the large view counts. That fits the filter. It does not prove the filter caused them.

A new-author boost for low-impression authors is documented, without the threshold. Paul's 32k-view essay is the small-account example. One essay.

## Method and limits

Native X search was available and supplied every post and follower count: `x_user_search`, `x_keyword_search` (Latest), `x_semantic_search`, `x_thread_fetch` on Paul's 12 Sep thread. Semantic search was mostly off-audience. The README was fetched because Tony's post points at it. `.agents/models.md` assigns this research to Grok 4.7. No subagents.

`min_faves` hides quiet posts. Oct 6 counts were still moving. Quotes inherit the parent. Role, newsletter, course, and product audiences confound the larger accounts. No follower deltas were observed. Nothing here says a format caused follows.
