## 1. Final positions

**R2 · Bonsai attachment stability (lead with J).** **Accept J's design** (five legacy roots frozen as versioned data, index sampling removed, `bonsai-shoots` schema unchanged) with the amendments below. Evidence. (a) `r3from-A.mjs` (M): today's every-third sampling yields exactly J's five roots; a pure 14-bit re-encode moves them 0.93–2.47 mm; every-vertex sampling would be order-independent (0 mm at 14 bits, ≤ 0.04 mm at 12) but changes today's roots by up to 2.2 mm, so freezing beats re-sampling. (b) **The round-2 "canopy re-arranges" claim is withdrawn.** A pure 14-bit re-encode with the frozen roots patched into the production engine chunk (`patch-roots.json`, applied through CDP; the twig buffers then match the shipping tree to 1e-7 units, the foliage likewise) still measured 1.42 % over 8 in the bonsai close-up and 1.67 % in the lantern close-up, the same as without the fix (1.46 / 1.66 %), while two controls agree to 0.000 % (M). Probing the running page (M): projection and camera rotation identical; **the camera eye moved** by (0.35, 0.14, 0.98) mm in the bonsai view and (1.1, 1.1, 1.6) mm in the lantern view. Cause, found by eliminating candidates one at a time in the running page (M): with the roots frozen, the foliage and twig buffers agree to 1e-7 units; restoring the shipped accessor `min`/`max` into the file (`fixbounds-A.mjs`, 103 accessors; Blender writes them from the unquantized mesh, a re-encode from the decoded vertices, ≤ 0.007 mm apart) makes every bounding box and the turntable pivot bit-identical, yet the eye still moves by the same amount. What remains is `viewOf()` itself (`engine.ts:917–921`): it fits the framing to every `count/600`-th vertex of each part, so a re-encode that reorders and dedups vertices (30,798 → 29,293 on the wood, 3,151 → 3,040 on the lantern frame) changes the sample and the six panning rounds settle 0.1–1.6 mm elsewhere; every lantern bar and the pot rim are outlined in `diffs3/`, the wide view (fixed camera) is at noise. The picking hulls sample every `count/4000`-th vertex the same way. Pinning the focus eye and target to the control's (harness patch, `pins.json`) proves the chain: the pure re-encode with frozen roots, restored bounds and a pinned camera is at the noise floor, **0.054 % over 8 in the bonsai close-up and 0.024 % in the lantern's** (pinned controls 0.040 / 0.020 %), and the full A1–A6 file measures **0.479 % / 0.229 %**, of which the albedo-only file accounts for 0.441 % / 0.229 % (texture grain on the wood, pot and plant leaves; the maximum errors of 67/62 are motes and the LED). So J was right that the causal link was unsupported; the roots move, but invisibly, and A3 is **not visually blocked** by them. (c) The baked `BonsaiTwigShadow` was built from today's twig tubes (`assets/3d/bonsai-foliage.json` → `build_scene.py:338–346`), one more reason to freeze rather than re-sample. **Amendments:** (1) name the file `src/lib/boot/assets/bonsai-roots.json`; `bonsai-foliage.json` already exists in `assets/3d/` as the Blender-side foliage export and must not be confused with it. (2) The coordinate frame is the wood mesh's local vertex frame in Blender millimetres (`woodToModel` reduces to the MM axis swap), so the packing script must never bake node transforms into BonsaiWood (no `flatten`/`join`/`dequantize` on it). (3) A12's validator: every root lies within 0.05 mm of a packed wood vertex (measured 0.0000 mm at 14 bits, ≤ 0.04 at 12), and the pack carries the exported POSITION bounds unchanged; a failure means the trunk was redesigned and needs an explicit `generatorVersion` decision, never a silent regeneration. (4) The provenance hash must be order-independent (sorted unique 14-bit vertex set), because any re-encode reorders and dedups (30,798 → 29,293 vertices). (5) `viewOf()` and the hull sampler should choose their points in a vertex-order-independent way (the box corners plus the neat foliage nodes, or a sorted sample): J's item, tiny, and it makes every future re-export leave the close-ups and hulls where they are; until then the acceptance harness pins the camera. **Owners:** J runtime, schema, storage validation and fixtures; A extraction, the validator, the bounds carry-through. **Acceptance:** J's identity tests (done this round, M: identical twig hashes and shoot metadata on original, 14-bit and permuted input; identical length arrays at 0/2/30 days) plus the camera-pinned render test: bonsai and lantern close-ups of the packed glb against the shipping glb at the noise floor except the owner-approved texture and simplification residue; saved trees replayed from the fixture. Sequence J0 before A3 for determinism, not for pixels.
