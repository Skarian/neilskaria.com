Room GLB: original vs latest c2s

Whole-model inspection views, rendered with Three.js. These are not Blender or app screenshots. Each pair uses the same camera (1600×1000, DPR 1, FOV 40°, framed on the original bounds). Every mesh in each GLB is rendered, with nothing hidden or moved.

Wide · three-quarter front right

High · overhead front

Inspection lighting: a neutral hemisphere light (2.2) and a directional light (1.6), with no tone mapping. The app's baked lightmaps are not applied, so these views look flatter than the app and do not show how the room looks in the app.

The parts missing from After (duvet, far pillow, cushion, bed legs, rug, left and side walls, window, far curtain, rod and sky) are the visibility trim. The app camera was measured never to see them. c2s also simplifies the sheet fold and the plant pot. Full SHA-256 values, cameras and method are in full-scene/NOTE.md.
