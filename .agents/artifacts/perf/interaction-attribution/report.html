What the other controls establish

Panel mounting: removing its work reduces the largest initial gaps; restoring it brings them back. Radio trace Layout 115→16ms, individual max 105→6ms. Cached bounds and empty animation keyframes alone fail to remove the stall.

Fitting: three calls (six rounds each) cost ~49–68ms at4×. With panel work held out, cached recorded views shorten first camera callbacks by ~67ms lantern /77ms radio. Radio preparation: noise filling and AudioContext creation are real click-handler costs; prepared contexts stay suspended until a trusted click. Outline: five first-use programs and reflection calls occur during mouse movement before the delivered click; omitting outline reduces that lead, while panel stalls remain.

Radio's later p95 gaps persist and can worsen even while its initial maximum improves. These controls establish contributors, not additive exclusive shares or a sole cause. Normal-CPU boot text has no severe tail on this host.
