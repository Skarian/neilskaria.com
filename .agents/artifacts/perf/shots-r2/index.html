Round 2 · A · before/after pairs

Hover over (or click) a picture to flip BEFORE → AFTER; the smaller picture beside it is the difference amplified 8×. BEFORE is the shipping site. AFTER swaps only the files named in the heading, injected at the browser's network layer: same build, same camera, same frozen time. Two control runs with nothing swapped differ by at most 5/255 in the close-ups and by a few motes and the clock LED in the wide views, so anything larger is the candidate. Frames are lossless WebP at the real pixel size (1280×800 desktop, 1081×2401 phone): view at 100%.
